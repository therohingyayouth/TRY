Empowering Rohingya Youth

Founded in 2025, The Rohingya Youth empowers Rohingya youth through education, advocacy, creativity, and community initiatives promoting their rights and well-being.

Our Activities

1. Youth Leadership & Empowerment

We create opportunities for Rohingya young people to develop leadership skills, confidence, and meaningful roles in their communities.

2. Education & Learning

We support learning, knowledge-sharing, and educational opportunities that help Rohingya youth develop skills for their futures.

3. Human Rights & Advocacy

We raise awareness of Rohingya rights, dignity, identity, and aspirations while creating opportunities for young people to participate in advocacy.

4. Community Organizing

We bring young people together through community activities, discussions, listening spaces, and youth-led initiatives.

5. Gender & Inclusion

We create meaningful spaces for young women and underrepresented voices to participate in leadership and dialogue.

6. Peacebuilding & Civic Engagement

We encourage youth participation in dialogue, peacebuilding, democracy, and discussions about an inclusive future.
