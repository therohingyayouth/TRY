About Us

The Rohingya Youth (TRY) is a dedicated community organization founded in 2025. We strive to create safe spaces, learning environments, and platforms for young Rohingya individuals to voice their aspirations, build leadership capacity, and foster peace and solidarity within the community.

Our Activities

Core pillars driving our community forward

01

Youth Leadership & Empowerment

We create opportunities for Rohingya young people to develop leadership skills, confidence, and meaningful roles in their communities.

02

Education & Learning

We support learning, knowledge-sharing, and educational opportunities that help Rohingya youth develop skills for their futures.

03

Human Rights & Advocacy

We raise awareness of Rohingya rights, dignity, identity, and aspirations while creating opportunities for youth advocacy.

04

Community Organizing

We bring young people together through community activities, discussions, listening spaces, and youth-led initiatives.

05

Gender & Inclusion

We create meaningful spaces for young women and underrepresented voices to participate in leadership and dialogue.

06

Peacebuilding & Civic Engagement

We encourage youth participation in dialogue, peacebuilding, democracy, and discussions about an inclusive future.
